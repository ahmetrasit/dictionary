B002 needs two bounded corrections before publication: its concept gloss conjunctively narrows alternative forms of expression or response difficulty, and its rendering of lu_010 overstates weakened speech as complete inability.
root_000145/B002 concept_gloss (major, high): The evidence presents failure to speak clearly, becoming unable to answer, and not knowing how to frame speech as alternatives. The gloss "kendini ifade edip cevap verememe" and its applicability instead combine expression failure and response failure into one cumulative boundary, excluding attested cases that realize only one alternative. Smallest correction: Change the gloss to an alternative formulation such as "kendini açıkça ifade edememe veya uygun cevap verememe" and revise the applicability and error profile so they no longer claim that both limitations must occur together.
root_000145/B002 lexical_glosses (major, high): The supplied sense says that the person becomes weak in speech because of weak understanding and thus becomes like a non-speaker. The rendering "zihinsel yetersizlik yüzünden konuşamaz hâle gelmek" converts weakness into total inability and removes the stated comparison. Smallest correction: Render lu_010 as "zihinsel güçsüzlük yüzünden konuşmada zayıflayıp konuşamayan biri gibi olmak" or an equally direct formulation that preserves both reduced ability and the comparison.
